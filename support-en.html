FAQ

  • Where is my data stored? All Mood Cat data is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to any server — record with confidence.
  • How do I back up my data? You can export a CSV or JSON file anytime from “Me → Data Management”. You can also protect your data with a full iCloud device backup.
  • How is a check-in streak calculated? Streaks count consecutive calendar days with at least one entry. If you miss a day, the streak resets the next day — your history is not affected.
  • How does the Mood Library open? It officially opens once you reach 200 total records. It contains eight collections, each gathering your daily entries for one type of emotion.
  • Will I lose data if I delete the app? Deleting the app removes all records on your device and cannot be undone. We recommend exporting your data regularly.

Data Export

From “Me → Data Management”, you can:

  • Export CSV — open with Excel / Numbers for easy reviewing and organizing
  • Export JSON — ideal for full backups or moving to a new device
  • Mood Card — create a beautiful share image; notes and other sensitive content are hidden automatically
